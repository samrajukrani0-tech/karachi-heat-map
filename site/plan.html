Quick estimate approximate

This uses a simple rule: serve the highest-priority area first, from the nearest stock point that still has supply, then the next. It runs on your phone and is usually close, but the exact planner can do better. The stock points you place are your own what-ifs, not verified relief centres.

Tap the map where your stock is kept, or pan the map and use the button. Up to five stock points.

    Darker areas receive a larger share of the stock. White areas receive nothing.

    What this cannot tell you
    • Need counts residents aged 60 and over plus children under 5, times 3 litres of drinking water a day (the Sphere Handbook survival figure) or one ORS sachet. The population layer counts about half the people the 2023 census found in Landhi, so real need is roughly 2.3 times higher. Read the shares, not the totals.
    • Distance is straight-line distance times 1.3, a stand-in for roads. It knows nothing about traffic, closures or which lanes a van can use.
    • It does not know what any centre actually holds. No relief centre has been verified in person yet.
    • Priority is a ranking within Landhi, not a verdict on any neighbourhood.

    Priority is a ranking within Landhi Town, not a measurement of risk on any absolute scale, and not a prediction of deaths. This is not an official warning system — for warnings follow the Pakistan Meteorological Department and PDMA Sindh.
