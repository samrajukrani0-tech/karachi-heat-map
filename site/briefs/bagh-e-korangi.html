What pushes these areas' scores up

  • Further from the nearest clinic or hospital (7 of 7 areas)
  • The ground here gets hotter on summer afternoons (3 of 7 areas)
  • Little greenery or shade (3 of 7 areas)
  • More people live in this area (1 of 7 areas)

Worth checking on the ground

  • Where do people go to cool down, and is there drinking water there?
  • How long are the power cuts here in the hot months?
  • Where do older people or young children live who may struggle in the heat?
  • Is the map wrong about anywhere here? That is the most useful thing to know.

What this cannot tell you. *People are modelled, and the model counts about half the people the 2023 census found in Landhi: compare areas, never read them as headcounts. Ground temperature is the surface, not the air, and says nothing about humidity. Power cuts are not included, although they are a large part of why heat harms people here. This describes areas, not the people in them, and is never a verdict on a neighbourhood.
Notes from the visit

Not an official warning system: for heatwave warnings follow the Pakistan Meteorological Department and PDMA Sindh. Priority is a ranking within Landhi Town, not a measure of risk on any absolute scale, and not a prediction of deaths. "Areas" are hexagons of about 0.1 km² within 500 m of the OpenStreetMap point.
