Karachi Heat Priority Map

This is a placeholder. The site is being built in the open and is not ready to be used for anything yet. The map, the data and the wording will all change.

What this will be

A free, mobile-first map that helps a Karachi relief team decide where heat-relief support should go first during a heatwave, and why: drinking water, oral rehydration salts, heat kits, cooling points and ambulance standby. The pilot area is Landhi Town.

What it is not

  • Not an official warning system. For warnings, follow the Pakistan Meteorological Department and PDMA Sindh.
  • Not a model of deaths, and not a claim about the cause of any death.
  • Not a verdict on any neighbourhood.

How it will work

Three things are mapped for each small area: how hot the ground gets on summer afternoons, how many people live there, and the circumstances that make heat more dangerous, such as age, dense building, little greenery and distance from care. These are combined into a single priority score, and every area shows the reasons behind its own score.
