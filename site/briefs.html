Field briefs

One printable A4 page for each named place in Landhi: which nearby areas are higher priority for heat support, why, how sure the model is, and what is worth checking on the ground. Each is under 1 MB, so it sends easily on WhatsApp.

A brief covers the areas within 500 m of the point OpenStreetMap marks for the place. It is not an official neighbourhood boundary, and OpenStreetMap names only some of Landhi, so many places have no brief yet.

Provisional. The weights are placeholders and distance to a relief centre has no data yet, so rankings may change. Every brief says so.
